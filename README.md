# Hi, I'm İrem Nur Zengin 👋

**Research Chemist · Analytical Characterization · ML-driven Molecular Property Prediction**

I'm a chemist and molecular biologist (M.Sc. Chemistry, B.Sc. Molecular Biology & Genetics) working at the intersection of **wet-lab analytics** and **computational chemistry**. I combine spectroscopic/chromatographic characterization with machine-learning potentials (ANI) and molecular modeling to predict the energy, conformation and interaction properties of molecules — and validate those predictions against experimental data.
